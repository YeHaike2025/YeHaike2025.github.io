Is there Gaussian Splatting support in Unreal Engine?

Not natively. As of Unreal Engine 5.8 there is no built-in 3D Gaussian Splatting renderer — any splat rendering comes from a plugin. And the plugins differ far more than their feature lists suggest: the implementation approach fixes your scene-size ceiling before anything else does.

The three approaches, and what each one costs you

ApproachScene size it survivesTrade-off
Niagara particle basedRoughly 100k–500k splatsInstalls in minutes, then breaks: sorting fails and the image shimmers once you grow
Native shader / custom renderer inside the pluginMillions and up, depending on the implementationThe usual balance point. Sorting, transparency and Lumen interaction still need care
Custom CUDA rendererHigh counts at high frame ratesWindows and NVIDIA only — no console, no mobile, no AMD

Free options come first, and that is not a sales line

Several free and open-source routes exist as of late 2026 — Niagara-based plugins, a CUDA-based open-source renderer, and the free plugins that capture-hardware vendors ship to support their scanners. None is uniformly better, but at small scene sizes the free tier is usually enough. Paying only makes sense once you hit a specific wall, and the wall is almost always scene size or shadows.

Six checks that decide whether it survives production

  • Sorting on camera move. Rotate the camera quickly. If splats pop or flicker, the depth sort is approximate and it will get worse with scene size.
  • Packaged builds. Test a packaged development build, not PIE. Shader and module loading differ, and this is where many splat plugins fail.
  • Shadow casting. Most splat renderers cannot write depth in a form shadow maps can use. If shadows matter, ask specifically.
  • Exposure and post-process. Check the splat against your project's exposure and colour grading; naive implementations look correct in a neutral scene and wrong in a graded one.
  • Interaction with Lumen and Nanite. Splats are transparent primitives. They do not participate in the same lighting path as your meshes.
  • File formats. .ply is the baseline. .spz and .sog are compressed formats that cut load times substantially — support for them tells you how actively the plugin is maintained.

Common questions

Does Unreal Engine 5.8 support Gaussian Splatting out of the box?

No. There is no built-in splat renderer in Unreal Engine 5.8. Epic has discussed adding one, but nothing has shipped, so all splat rendering today is third-party.

Why do Gaussian splats flicker or sort incorrectly?

Because splats are transparent primitives with no depth write — correct rendering needs a per-frame depth sort, and most lightweight implementations approximate it. Flicker when the camera moves is a sorting problem, not a GPU problem.

Can splats cast shadows in Unreal Engine?

Usually not directly. Most renderers cannot produce depth in a form shadow maps consume, so shadows are either unsupported or faked with proxy meshes.
